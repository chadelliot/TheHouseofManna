## Program alignment without overstating operations
1. **General Operations:** first funding priority; warehouse/refrigeration/utilities/staffing remain proposed capacity needs. Keep the existing 2027 $75,000 planning campaign distinct from the 2026 Pantry and Youth Innovation Lab goals. No lease, asset, purchase, or results should be represented as secured unless documented.
2. **Pantry:** current core food-access work; a goal of 50 families per month is a target, not an assertion of measured distribution. Pantry and potential mobile distribution require distinct substantiated costs.
3. **Youth Innovation Lab / Academy:** use only for eligible, defined education, workforce, AI or entrepreneurship activities; don't describe pilot plans or future partners as ongoing activities without operational evidence.
4. **Commerce Engine:** do not create a new unbudgeted program category inside the grant tracker. Evaluate prospects under existing Youth Innovation Lab or General Operations only where the actual request matches a documented eligible program.
