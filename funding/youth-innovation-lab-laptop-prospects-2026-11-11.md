## Rules

- Never label an application Submitted until a portal submission, email send or equivalent receipt exists.
- School-eligible grants may require Phoenix Academy to apply itself; confirm their permission and applicant role.
- Do not write that all 25 devices will be delivered to eight students or invent an expansion cohort. Verify the plan with school.
- Collect device specifications, acceptable refurbished condition, security, chargers, warranty, asset ownership and replacement support before receiving devices.
- Amounts are intentionally unset. No grant budget has been changed.
