# House of Manna Document Brand Style

**Status:** Canonical standard for all House of Manna grant, funding, program, proposal, LOI, case-for-support, budget narrative, and related external documents.

**Primary visual references:**  
1. MacFarlane LOI  
2. Giant Food Program Overview  

**Supporting reference variants:**  
3. Regeneron LOI  
4. House of Manna - Abell LOI  

The goal is not to make future documents "similar." Future documents should look like members of the same document family.

## Non-negotiable design rule

For every House of Manna document, preserve the established visual identity unless the user explicitly requests a different design. Content may change; the brand system should not.

## Page architecture

- Page size: US Letter, 612 x 792 pt (8.5 x 11 in).
- Standard left/right content margin: 54 pt (0.75 in).
- Standard usable content width: 504 pt.
- Favor generous white space and strong vertical rhythm over dense layouts.
- Use thin rules and restrained section dividers rather than heavy boxes.
- Keep cover/first pages visually open; avoid filling all available space.
- Use a consistent footer separated by a thin horizontal rule.
- Footer should identify the document/funder on the left or upper footer line and the program/document context on the right, with House of Manna + website beneath when space permits.

## Canonical colors

- **House Navy:** #2D3B4F - primary headline, body emphasis, table totals, major dark blocks.
- **House Sage:** #6C7A63 - section headings, kicker text, funding-request emphasis, accent rules.
- **Body Gray:** #52565C - subtitles and softer secondary text.
- **Metadata Gray:** #83888F - secondary labels where needed.
- **Pale Sage:** #8E9C85 - secondary accent.
- **Warm Cream:** #F7F4EC - restrained alternating-row or highlight fill.
- **Light Gray:** #DDE0E3 - rules, table headers/fills, card outlines.
- **White:** #FFFFFF - dominant page background.

Do not introduce new colors casually. Funder colors should not replace House of Manna colors.

## Typography

### Primary display headline
- Font: **Anton Regular**
- Color: House Navy
- Case: uppercase
- Typical size:
  - 24 pt for longer two-line titles
  - 33 pt for shorter hero titles
- Tight, bold, editorial, condensed presence.
- Do not use generic bold sans-serif as a substitute when Anton is available.

### Section headings
- Font: **Anton Regular**
- Color: House Sage
- Typical size: 12.75 pt
- Uppercase, visibly letter-spaced.
- Usually paired with a thin light-gray horizontal rule extending to the right.

### Document kicker
Examples: "Letter of Inquiry", "Program Overview"
- Font: **Times Italic** / classic italic serif treatment matching the references.
- Color: House Sage
- Typical size: 16.5 pt
- Title case, not uppercase.
- Used as a quiet editorial signal above the main title.

### Funding-request line
- Font: **Anton Regular**
- Color: House Sage
- Typical size: 15 pt
- Uppercase / tracked.
- Keep visually prominent but smaller than the main title.

### Body copy
- Font: **Helvetica**
- Color: House Navy
- Typical size:
  - 10.5 pt for compact program-overview pages
  - 11.25 pt for formal LOI narrative pages
- Use comfortable leading and short-to-medium paragraph blocks.
- Avoid walls of text.

### Bold body / labels
- Font: **Helvetica Bold**
- Color: House Navy
- Used for salutations, RE labels, callout labels, card labels, and table totals.

### Metadata labels
Examples: SUBMISSION DATE, SUBMITTED TO
- Font: **Helvetica Bold**
- Color: House Sage
- Typical size: 9 pt
- Uppercase and tracked.

### Metadata values
- Font: **Helvetica**
- Color: House Navy
- Typical size: 10.5 pt

## First-page LOI layout

Use the MacFarlane and Regeneron LOIs as the canonical composition.

- Kicker at x ≈ 54 pt.
- Hero headline begins at x ≈ 54 pt.
- House of Manna logo sits top-right:
  - x ≈ 415.5 to 558 pt
  - width ≈ 142.5 pt
  - preserve proportions.
- Subtitle directly beneath headline in Helvetica 10.5 / Body Gray.
- Funding-request line beneath subtitle in Anton / House Sage.
- Metadata is split into columns below a thin horizontal rule.
- Full-width 3 pt House Sage divider separates hero/metadata from narrative.
- Narrative begins at x = 54 pt.
- Section headings use Anton Sage + horizontal rule.

## Program Overview first-page layout

Use Giant Food Program Overview as the canonical composition.

- Editorial kicker + large two-line Anton headline on left.
- House of Manna logo top-right.
- Short gray subtitle below headline.
- Full-width sage divider below hero.
- Compact content modules with section-heading rules.
- When a process is useful, use four rounded white cards with:
  - large Anton numeric step in sage,
  - bold Helvetica step name,
  - short Helvetica explanation.
- Keep cards evenly sized with subtle light-gray outlines/shadows.
- Use a clean two-column fact list for compact supporting information.

## Outcome cards

Use the Regeneron page-2 treatment when metrics materially help.

- Rounded white cards with subtle light-gray outline/shadow.
- Large Anton metric/value in House Navy.
- Smaller Helvetica label/body beneath.
- Even spacing and equal card heights.
- Do not fabricate metrics; projections must be clearly framed as targets.

## Tables and budgets

Use MacFarlane and Regeneron budget tables as the primary model.

- Rounded outer container when practical.
- Light Gray (#DDE0E3) table header.
- Header labels: small bold uppercase House Navy.
- Body rows: white with thin Light Gray separators.
- Expense description left aligned.
- Dollar value right aligned and bold.
- Final total row: House Navy background with white text.
- Total amount may use a larger Anton treatment for emphasis.
- Cream (#F7F4EC) may be used sparingly for alternating/highlight rows, as in the Abell reference.
- Avoid excessive gridlines.

## Footer system

- Thin light-gray rule above footer.
- Footer uses small Helvetica text.
- House of Manna, Inc. and houseofmannamd.com should appear consistently.
- Document/funder label is uppercase and tracked when space permits.
- Program/document title may sit on the right.
- Page numbers are appropriate for program overviews and longer attachments.
- Keep footer understated.

## White-space and density rules

- Default left/right margin: 0.75 in.
- Avoid squeezing body text to fill a page.
- Preserve visible space between:
  - hero and metadata,
  - metadata and narrative,
  - paragraphs,
  - section headings and body copy,
  - tables and surrounding narrative,
  - closing/signature and footer.
- It is acceptable for a page to contain substantial unused white space if that preserves hierarchy.
- Never shrink text aggressively merely to avoid a page break.

## Voice + visual alignment

The visual system should reinforce:
- professional,
- calm,
- credible,
- modern,
- community-centered,
- operationally disciplined.

Avoid:
- generic nonprofit templates,
- funder-branded redesigns,
- loud gradients,
- decorative clip art,
- excessive icon use,
- dense page filling,
- novelty fonts,
- arbitrary color changes.

## Canonical archetype selection

- **Formal LOI / Letter / Inquiry:** MacFarlane is the primary template.
- **Shorter LOI with visual metrics:** Regeneron is the primary variant.
- **Program overview / support attachment:** Giant Food is the primary template.
- **Long-form LOI / proposal with alternating budget highlights:** Abell is the supporting variant.
- **Budget-only attachment:** use MacFarlane/Regeneron table language and footer system.

## Quality-control requirement

Before delivering any House of Manna document:
1. Compare typography to the canonical references.
2. Compare colors to the canonical palette.
3. Confirm 0.75 in content margins unless a deliberate reference-based variation is needed.
4. Verify logo scale and placement.
5. Verify section-heading treatment.
6. Verify footer treatment.
7. Verify white-space balance.
8. Render the PDF and visually inspect every page for clipping, collisions, awkward breaks, or off-brand substitutions.
9. If a requested exact font is unavailable, use the closest metric-compatible substitute only as a last resort and disclose the substitution.
10. Never share font files with the user.

**Standing instruction:** If future House of Manna document instructions are ambiguous, preserve this brand system by default.
