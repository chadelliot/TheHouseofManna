A $150 gift card contribution adds practical support

An in-kind gift card contribution helps the organization prepare for community-serving activities.

Published November 11, 2026Community Support

Some contributions are modest in size but especially useful in practice. The House of Manna recently received a gift card valued at $150 to help support our community-serving work.

In-kind support such as gift cards can help offset eligible supplies and other practical program needs. We will account for the contribution as a noncash gift and document any resulting purchases.

Every contribution matters

We are not publicly identifying the supporting retailer. We appreciate the generosity behind this gift and the role that local community support can play in helping a growing nonprofit serve its neighbors.

This update recognizes a contribution already received; it should not be read as a report of supplies already purchased or distributed.

Support the mission

We welcome appropriate in-kind donations, program supplies and community partnerships.
