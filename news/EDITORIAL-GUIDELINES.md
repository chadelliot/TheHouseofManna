# The House of Manna — News publishing rules

- Add each milestone as an individual dated HTML post with canonical, OpenGraph and social share metadata. Add a card to `news.html` and link relevant program pages to the post. Use the published date (not a fabricated original award date).
- Never identify students, share identifiable images, or disclose private student details without appropriate permissions. Present only confirmed enrollment as confirmed; do not describe planned sessions as completed.
- Name a donor, grantmaker, corporate supporter or retailer **only after there is explicit evidence that public acknowledgment is authorized**. Otherwise describe the gift by type/value without naming or identifying clues. Do not imply that a donor is legally anonymous just because they were not named publicly.
- Never convert a gift card into a cash award in public reporting. Distinguish amounts received, awarded, pledged, applied for, spent and distributed.
- Keep the authoritative internal record in `funding/grants.json`; public news is not the grant-status system or financial ledger. Verify dates/amounts before publication.
- For school partnerships and photos, confirm publication permission separately; do not assume an existing image is cleared for external fundraising uses.
- Any program fundraising CTA should link to the specific Innovation Lab campaign or the contact page rather than the pantry campaign.
- News items published November 11, 2026: first eight confirmed students, $5,000 unnamed contribution already received, and a previously received $150 gift card with supporter unnamed. Do not claim award dates from publication dates.
